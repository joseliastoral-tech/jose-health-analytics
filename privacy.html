Privacy Policy

Jose Health Analytics is a personal-use application designed to analyze health, fitness, recovery, sleep, and performance data obtained from WHOOP.

Data Usage

WHOOP data accessed by this application is used solely by the account owner for personal health, fitness, and performance analysis.

Data Sharing

This application does not sell, distribute, or share WHOOP data with third parties.

Data Access

Access to WHOOP data is authorized by the user through WHOOP's authorization system and can be revoked by the user.

Purpose

The application is intended for personal analytics and educational purposes only.
